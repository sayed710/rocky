# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: individual chess players worldwide. Beginners and improving players need a calm, clear way into the product; competitive and rated players still expect fairness, precision, reliability, and responsive play under time pressure. English and Arabic matter, without making the product culturally narrow or institution-first.

## Product Purpose

Rookzen is a complete chess platform for playing, analysing, training, improving, and participating in a community without unnecessary complexity. AI coaching is one useful capability inside that journey, not the product's identity or sole differentiator.

## Positioning

A global, player-first chess platform that keeps the path to play obvious and reveals learning, analysis, competition, and community depth progressively.

## Brand Personality

Calm, welcoming, clear, intelligent, and modern. The visual direction is dark-first Burgundy & Stone: warm enough to invite play, restrained enough to keep the board and the player's current task in control.

## Anti-references

Two things to explicitly avoid: the generic SaaS-dashboard look (cards everywhere, gradient heroes, tool-for-work aesthetics — this is a game, not a B2B app), and the cluttered/gamified chess-site look (busy badges, streak counters, and noisy gamification competing with the board for attention).

## Design Principles

- **Play first.** The route into a real game is immediately legible without pretending that guest play exists when the service contracts require authentication.
- **Progressive disclosure.** Keep every supported feature reachable while grouping deeper learning, analysis, and community paths behind clear entry points.
- **The board is the product.** Every game screen should feel like it exists in service of the position and the player's next action — nothing decorative competes with it.
- **Precision over decoration.** Visual choices should read as considered craft (tight spacing, deliberate typography, restrained color), not gamified noise or trend-chasing flourish.
- **Trust through polish.** Competitive players judge fairness and seriousness by how tight the UI feels — janky transitions or inconsistent states erode trust as much as a real bug would.
- **Timeless over trendy.** Favor choices that will still look premium in five years over whatever reads as "modern" this quarter.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA baseline (matching the existing Lighthouse a11y ≥ 0.95 CI gate), keyboard-visible focus, 44px touch targets on coarse pointers, and colorblind-safe board cues. English is current; Arabic/RTL support must be introduced only with real localized content and tested mirroring rather than decorative assumptions.
